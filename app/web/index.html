Click the view to take control, then move the mouse to look around — the G1 turns to face wherever you are looking. Hold W and it hauls itself that way up the fixed line; let go and it just hangs on the ascender, standing in the wind. Esc releases the pointer. R respawns the climber and puts the camera back behind it, without giving up the pointer.
